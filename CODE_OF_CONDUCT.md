# Code of Conduct

WorldFoundry welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience
levels. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
community channels, and events where participants represent WorldFoundry.

## Expected Conduct

- Treat people with respect and use welcoming, inclusive language.
- Discuss technical work constructively; criticize ideas, not people.
- Respect different viewpoints, accept feedback, and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy, consent, and contributors' time.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
sustained disruption, and publishing private information without consent are
not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to a maintainer identified in
[CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS), using a direct message through the community
Slack or Discord linked in [README.md](README.md#-community). Include the
relevant links, what happened, and any immediate safety concerns. Avoid
posting personal information in public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review reports fairly and handle identifying information privately,
sharing it only as needed to investigate and respond. A maintainer involved in
an incident must not decide the outcome of that report.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may request a correction, issue a
warning, remove content, restrict participation temporarily, or exclude a
participant from project spaces. Participants may request a review of an
enforcement decision by an uninvolved maintainer.
