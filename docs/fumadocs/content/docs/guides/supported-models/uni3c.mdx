Uni3C from Alibaba DAMO Academy unifies camera control and human-motion control for image-to-video generation: the PCDController stage steers the camera while the unified stage combines camera plus human motion (RealisDance-DiT). WorldFoundry vendors both official stage-two inference paths in-tree, with public Apache-2.0 checkpoints (ewrfcas/Uni3C, theFoxofSky/RealisDance-DiT) on top of the Wan2.1-I2V-14B-720P base.

## Architecture

Three checkpoint roles are recorded: ewrfcas/Uni3C as pcd_controller, Wan-AI/Wan2.1-I2V-14B-720P-Diffusers as camera_base, and theFoxofSky/RealisDance-DiT as the unified camera-plus-human model. Pre-rendered control bundles stay explicit runtime inputs; preprocessing is not silently substituted.

## Runtime contract

Choose the camera-only or unified camera-plus-human route and provide the matching pre-rendered control bundle. The default PCDController camera route is checkpoint-backed and GPU-validated on H100; the unified camera-plus-human route still requires its own checkpoint-backed validation.

## Evaluation benchmarks

- [CameraBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/camerabench) — Camera-trajectory adherence under explicit control.

## Readiness and limitations

- The recorded GPU validation covers the default PCDController camera route, not the unified camera-plus-human route.
- Control bundles must be pre-rendered; the pipeline does not generate them.
