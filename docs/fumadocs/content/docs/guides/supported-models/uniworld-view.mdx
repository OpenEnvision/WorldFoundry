## Architecture

Official UniView transformer on Wan2.1-VACE-14B with geometry estimation and rendered camera controls.

## Runtime contract

Use a pinned official checkout with STream3R under extern, a separate upstream environment and the official local weights. The upstream CLI uses BLIP2 captions rather than a user text prompt.

## Readiness and limitations

- The upstream authors recommend at least 60 GB of GPU memory.
- Validation covers specific official samples and settings; quantitative camera-angle accuracy and other scenes remain unmeasured.
- With aspect-ratio preservation enabled, actual output dimensions can differ from the requested height and width.
