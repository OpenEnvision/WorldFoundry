UniAnimate-DiT is Alibaba ali-vilab's human image animation model built on Wan2.1-I2V: it animates a person photo to follow a driving pose sequence. The in-tree route is wired with local checkpoint assets and exposes max_frames, resolution, step count, and seed controls.

## Architecture

Built on the Wan2.1 image-to-video diffusion transformer with pose-sequence conditioning for human animation (checkpoint: ZheWang123/UniAnimate-DiT). Deeper conditioning internals are not recorded in this repository.

## Runtime contract

A checkpoint-backed short GPU smoke test passed; full runner parity is pending. Prepare a clean subject photo and a driving pose sequence for best results.

## Readiness and limitations

- A nine-frame, four-step GPU smoke artifact has been verified; official 81-frame, 50-step quality and pose-control parity have not been measured.
- Checkpoints are external assets — missing weights are reported at runtime rather than downloaded automatically.
- Specialized for human subjects — not a general I2V model.
