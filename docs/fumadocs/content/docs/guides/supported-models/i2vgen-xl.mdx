I2VGen-XL is Alibaba ali-vilab's cascaded image-to-video model, one of the earlier high-resolution open I2V releases. It turns a still image (with optional text) into a short video through a two-stage generate-then-refine cascade. The WorldFoundry route is complete and reports missing checkpoint assets at runtime.
