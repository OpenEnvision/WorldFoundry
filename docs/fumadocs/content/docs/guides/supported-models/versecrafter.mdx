## What it is for

Use `versecrafter` for camera-conditioned video inference. The recipe binds the recorded camera-control profile.

Two stages are wired in-tree: camera-only 4D-control rendering followed by the diffusion stage. The diffusion backbone comes from the pinned VideoX-Fun submodule. Further architecture internals are not recorded in this repository. It is published by Tencent ARC Lab.

Catalog tasks include camera-controlled-video, image-to-video, and world-model. Documented modalities: image in, video out.

Recorded evaluation entry points include [CameraBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/camerabench) (Camera-path adherence for 4D-controlled generation).
