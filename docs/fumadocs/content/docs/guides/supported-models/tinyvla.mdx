**Publisher:** Open-source research release (institution not recorded) · Research lab

TinyVLA targets fast, data-efficient VLA at sub-billion scale: LLaVA-Pythia backbones (400M / 700M / 1.3B released on the Hub) with ACT or conditional 1D-diffusion action heads. The published Hub models are VLM backbones for policy training — not task-trained robot checkpoints — so the in-tree runtime is checkpoint-gated until you stage a trained task policy.

## Architecture

LLaVA-Pythia multimodal backbones with CLIP/SigLIP vision fusion and swappable action heads (ACT or conditional 1D diffusion), executed fully in process. Both merged weights and local LoRA plus non-LoRA action tensors are supported.

## Runtime contract

Runtime construction fails early until a compatible task checkpoint, its config.json (action dimensions and head type), and dataset statistics are staged locally.

## Readiness and limitations

- No public task-trained action checkpoint; released Hub models are backbones only.
- Mixed MIT/Apache-2.0 licensing across components.
