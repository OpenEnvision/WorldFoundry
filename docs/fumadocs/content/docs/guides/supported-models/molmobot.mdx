**Publisher:** Allen Institute for AI (AI2) · Research lab

MolmoBot is AI2's robot policy family around the Molmo VLM, with Apache-2.0 checkpoints for Franka DROID, image-conditioned DROID, RBY1 multitask, and a Pi0-based DROID variant. WorldFoundry records the OLMo/MolmoBot-Pi0 action inference contracts and calls a flattened in-tree inference wrapper.
