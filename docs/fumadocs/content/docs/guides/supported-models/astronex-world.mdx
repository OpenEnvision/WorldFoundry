## What it is for

Use `astronex-world` for camera-conditioned video inference. The recipe binds the recorded camera-control profile.

Wan2.2 TI2V-5B with PRoPE camera conditioning, 64-dimensional actions and embodiment conditioning. The official sampler remains external; no Wan backbone is copied into this integration. It is published by Astronex-Robotics.

Catalog tasks include text-to-video, image-to-video, camera-controlled-video, and interactive-world-model. Catalog aliases include `astronex`. Documented modalities: text and image in, video out.

## Runtime contract
