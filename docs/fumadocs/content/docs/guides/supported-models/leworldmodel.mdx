**Publisher:** Mila – Quebec AI Institute / Université de Montréal · University

LeWorldModel (le-wm) studies latent world models: JEPA-style and pixel-space world models trained and evaluated on control tasks such as PushT. In this catalog an infer-only wrapper for the official eval route is registered; execution needs stable-worldmodel packages, task datasets, and policy checkpoints when the policy is not random.

## Architecture

The recorded structure spans latent (JEPA) and pixel world-model variants (checkpoints at eren23/lewm-models). Runtime eval configs live under data/models/runtime/configs/le_wm/config/eval; train configs are not required for inference.

## Runtime contract

The route matches the official eval.py config flow and reports missing stable-worldmodel packages, task datasets, or non-random policy checkpoints before execution. Official PushT evaluation parity remains pending.

## Readiness and limitations

- Evaluation-oriented world model: it scores dynamics, it does not render showcase video.
- Official PushT evaluation parity is pending.
