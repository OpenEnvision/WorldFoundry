Causal-rCM applies NVIDIA's rCM consistency distillation to causal video generation: it streams video autoregressively over latent-frame chunks with distilled few-step sampling, making it a low-latency interactive world-model candidate. In this catalog the official Apache-2.0 causal inference stack is vendored in-tree; execution waits on the public DiT, Wan2.1 VAE, and umT5 checkpoints being staged locally.

## What it is for

`causal-rcm` is the recorded interactive world line. Start here when the job is stepping a world with actions.

Per the paper (arXiv 2510.08431), rCM combines score-regularized consistency distillation for few-step sampling. The vendored stack drops the upstream imaginaire framework — WorldFoundry's own lazy_config, easy_io, and logging back it — and the block-causal mask predicates were lifted into worldfoundry/core/attention/sparse/block_pattern.py, verified against upstream across 702 mask and 162 chunk-schedule configurations.

Catalog tasks include autoregressive-video-generation, streaming-video-generation, interactive-world-model, and text-to-video. Catalog aliases include `rcm`, `causal_rcm`, `rcm-causal`, and `causal-rcm-wan2.1`. Documented modalities: text and image in, video out.

Recorded evaluation entry points include [VBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/vbench) (Video quality evaluation for streaming text/image-to-video).

## Runtime contract

Required `prompt` and `image`; optional `video` and `actions`; artifacts `generated_world`, `generated_video`, and `causal-rcm.mp4`.

Recorded task profiles: `autoregressive-video-generation`, `streaming-video-generation`, `interactive-world-model`, `text-to-video`, and `image-to-video`. WorldFoundry binds `CausalRCMPipeline`.

Family status: **Runner parity pending**.

first_chunk_t and chunk_t select the schedule: c1-1 frame-wise gives the lowest streaming latency, c3-3 more throughput. Distilled 4-step sampling is the default; distilled=False samples the multi-step teacher with CFG. context_from_last_step=True saves one clean forward per chunk but requires a checkpoint trained for noisy context. The released entrypoint is prompt/image conditioned with no discrete action space.

WorldFoundry vendors the official Apache-2.0 causal inference stack under synthesis/visual_generation/rcm/rcm_runtime; the adapter owns checkpoint gating, command construction, and pipeline/catalog exposure.

## Running in WorldFoundry

Launch `worldfoundry-eval run causal-rcm --pipeline.task-profile autoregressive-video-generation` to step the world. The command builder above switches `--pipeline.task-profile` among recorded tasks and still emits `worldfoundry-eval run`.

Recorded weight repositories include `worstcoder/rcm-Wan` and `worstcoder/Wan`. Stage assets with `bash scripts/inference/prepare_model_infer.sh causal-rcm`.

Status: **Runner parity pending**.
