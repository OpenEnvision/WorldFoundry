MMAudio generates soundtracks for silent video: given a video (and optionally a text prompt), it synthesizes audio that is semantically and temporally aligned with the visuals. In this catalog it is the companion tool for adding audio to clips produced by the video models — its artifact type is generated_audio, not video.
