## Architecture

Per the upstream publication (ICCV 2023), a canonical-camera transformation resolves the metric ambiguity of single-image depth, and the recorded checkpoint is JUGGHM/Metric3D under a bsd-2-clause license. Registered at base_models/three_dimensions/depth/metric3d.

## Runtime contract

It is integrated as an independent geometry prior under worldfoundry/base_models and exposed through the shared geometry-prior pipeline/operator contract. Higher-level pipelines (e.g. ViPE) import it from this canonical owner instead of keeping private copies. Runner parity is verified through the shared contract; it is the default metric-depth choice in the VIPE SLAM configuration.

## Readiness and limitations

- Metric accuracy depends on camera-intrinsics quality; wrong intrinsics degrade the scale.
- Single-image prior; temporal stability on video is out of scope.
