DB-CogACT is the CogACT recipe rebuilt inside Dexmal's Dexbotic framework, with an unusually complete released checkpoint zoo: single-arm and dual-arm foundations plus task checkpoints for LIBERO, CALVIN, and SimplerEnv, all Apache-2.0. WorldFoundry constructs the official Dexbotic policy in-process from a vendored inference subset and emits LIBERO action traces without an external checkout.

## What it is for

`db-cogact-libero` is the recorded instruction-to-action line. Start here when you need a WorldFoundry VLA rollout rather than a video generator.

The Dexbotic v1 inference contract is recorded: requests carry observation.prompt and observation images, and the policy follows the CogACT VLM-plus-DiT-action design re-implemented in Dexbotic. The vendored infer subset lives under worldfoundry/synthesis/action_generation/db_cogact/db_cogact_runtime.

Catalog tasks include vla, vla action prediction, and vla policy rollout. Catalog aliases include `dexbotic`, `db_cogact`, and `dexbotic-cogact`.

Recorded evaluation entry points include [LIBERO](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/libero) (Dedicated libero-db-cogact checkpoint), [CALVIN](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/calvin) (Dedicated calvin-db-cogact checkpoint), [SimplerEnv](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/simpler-env) (Dedicated simpler-db-cogact checkpoint).

DB-CogACT LIBERO specialist. Broader CogACT is the sibling.

## Runtime contract

Optional `prompt`, `input_path`, `interactions`, `fps`, and `timeout_seconds`; artifacts `action_trace` and `db-cogact_action_trace.json`.

CLI task profiles: `default`. WorldFoundry binds `DBCogACTPipeline`.

Family status: **Runner parity pending**.

Pick the checkpoint matching your benchmark: libero-db-cogact, calvin-db-cogact, or simpler-db-cogact; SArm/HArm foundations serve fine-tuning. Integration status is Runner parity pending.

WorldFoundry records the Dexbotic v1 inference contract with an independent DB-CogACT synthesis entrypoint.

## Running in WorldFoundry

Launch `worldfoundry-eval run db-cogact-libero --pipeline.task-profile default` to execute the policy. The command builder above switches `--pipeline.task-profile` among recorded tasks and still emits `worldfoundry-eval run`.

It runs in the unified environment `worldfoundry-unified-cu128` (Python 3.11, CUDA 12.8, torch>=2.6.0).

Recorded weight repositories include `Dexmal/libero-db-cogact`, `Dexmal/Dexbotic-CogACT-SArm`, and `Dexmal/Dexbotic-CogACT-HArm` among others. Prepare the environment and check local assets with `bash scripts/inference/prepare_model_infer.sh db-cogact-libero`. Add `--download` to fetch missing public checkpoints.

Recorded licenses include apache-2.0; status: **Runner parity pending**.
