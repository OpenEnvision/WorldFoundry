Wan2.2-Fun A14B Control Camera is the largest Wan2.2-generation camera-control checkpoint in the VideoX-Fun family: an A14B (mixture-of-experts style) diffusion backbone steered by explicit camera pose sequences for image-to-video. WorldFoundry vendors VideoX-Fun in-tree (revision 403f1f7b) and wires a dedicated route for alibaba-pai/Wan2.2-Fun-A14B-Control-Camera under Apache-2.0.

## What it is for

Pick `wan22-fun-a14b-cam` when you need camera-controlled video: a camera trajectory in, a steered clip out. Use it instead of unconstrained T2V when viewpoint must follow a path.

An A14B Wan2.2 diffusion backbone with the VideoX-Fun Control-Camera conditioning pathway. Further internals are not recorded in this repository. It is published by Alibaba PAI (VideoX-Fun).

Catalog tasks include camera-controlled-video and image-to-video. Documented modalities: image in, video out.

Recorded evaluation entry points include [CameraBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/camerabench) (Camera-pose adherence for controlled i2v generation).

Not [Wan2.2](/docs/guides/supported-models/wan2.2) and [Wan2.2 Fun 5B Cam](/docs/guides/supported-models/wan22-fun-5b-cam). Score on [CameraBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/camerabench).

Family status: **Runner parity pending**.

Reserve this variant for maximum-quality camera-controlled generation once your pipeline is proven on the GPU-validated 1.3B sibling; A14B checkpoints demand the largest VRAM budget in the family. Runner parity is pending.

## Running in WorldFoundry

The catalog launch is `worldfoundry-eval run wan22-fun-a14b-cam --pipeline.task-profile default`. The command builder above switches `--pipeline.task-profile` among recorded tasks and still emits `worldfoundry-eval run`.

It runs in the unified environment `worldfoundry-unified-cu128` (Python 3.11, CUDA 12.8).

Recorded weight repositories include `alibaba-pai/Wan2.2-Fun-A14B-Control-Camera`. Prepare the environment and check local assets with `bash scripts/inference/prepare_model_infer.sh wan22-fun-a14b-cam`. Add `--download` to fetch missing public checkpoints.
