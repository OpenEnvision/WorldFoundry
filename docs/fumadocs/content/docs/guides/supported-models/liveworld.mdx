LiveWorld is a camera-controlled video-to-video world model with a monitor-centric workflow: it consumes a video plus a geometry NPZ and re-renders the scene under new camera behavior. In this catalog the official source is vendored at a pinned revision and the dedicated geometry-NPZ route is wired; prediction waits on four missing checkpoint snapshots.

## What it is for

Pick `liveworld` when you need camera-controlled video: a camera trajectory in, a steered clip out. Use it instead of unconstrained T2V when viewpoint must follow a path.

The recorded stack pins Wan2.1-T2V-14B and SAM3, both structurally validated locally (apache-2.0). Architecture internals beyond the recorded structure are not stored in this repository.

Catalog tasks include camera-controlled-video, video-to-video, and world-model. Documented modalities: video in, video out.

Recorded evaluation entry points include [CameraBench](/docs/evaluation/benchmark-hub/camerabench) (Camera-behavior adherence of re-rendered video).

## Running in WorldFoundry

Launch `worldfoundry-eval run liveworld --pipeline.task-profile default` to follow the camera path. The command builder above switches `--pipeline.task-profile` among recorded tasks and still emits `worldfoundry-eval run`.

It runs in the unified environment `worldfoundry-unified-cu128` (Python 3.11, CUDA 12.8).

Recorded weight repositories include `ZichengD/LiveWorld`, `Wan-AI/Wan2.1-T2V-14B`, and `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct` among others. Prepare the environment and check local assets with `bash scripts/inference/prepare_model_infer.sh liveworld`. Add `--download` to fetch missing public checkpoints.
