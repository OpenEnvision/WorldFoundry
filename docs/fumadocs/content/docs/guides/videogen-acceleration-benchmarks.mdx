WorldFoundry ships one unified inference acceleration stack across **15 foundation models** (11 flagship video generation models plus 4 real-time autoregressive / interactive world models). Every reported tier pairs auditable zero-fallback runtime receipts (`fallbacks: []`, `capture_failed: 0`, `dense_fallback_calls: 0`) with direct visual inspection of rendered RGB frames.

## Methodology and Hardware Disclosures

- **Hardware and Software Stack**: Bare-metal **NVIDIA H200 SXM (141 GiB HBM3e, SM90, NVSwitch)**, PyTorch `2.11.0+cu128`, CUDA 12.8, Triton 3.6.0, Hopper FlashAttention-3 `3.0.1`, SM90 CUTLASS `sol_attn`, and in-tree Hopper TMA `PISA`.
- **Contention Separation**:
  - **Clean**: Measured on exclusive GPUs with zero competing processes (`11 of 15` models plus `1x H200 LTX-2.5`).
  - **Paired**: On `Wan2.2-TI2V-5B`, `Wan2.2-T2V-A14B`, `HunyuanVideo-13B`, and `2x H200 LTX-2.5 CFG=2`, a background training job shared the host (~33.5 GiB/GPU). Baseline `L0` and accelerated tiers ran back-to-back on the exact same GPU(s); we report **paired relative speedups (`L0 / L_opt`)** and do not claim contended wall-clock seconds as exclusive latency. Cross-checks on `SANA-Video` (`2.862x` contended vs `2.836x` clean) and `LingBot-Video` (`3.059x` contended vs `3.069x` clean) verified under `1%` ratio variance.
- **Hardware Precision Caveat**: Where NVIDIA SOL Engine uses Blackwell **B200 SM100 NVFP4**, WorldFoundry runs **Hopper SM90 Rowwise FP8 W8A8 (`use_fast_accum=False` full FP32 accumulation)** on H200. SM100 NVFP4 weight packing and dispatch are implemented and unit-tested, pending physical B200 validation.

## Cross-Framework Comparison and Honest Trade-Offs

- **Where WorldFoundry leads**: Unified breadth across **15 models** in one open-source codebase; lossless BF16 streaming world models (**30–67.5 FPS** with `O(1)` graph segments); auditable zero-fallback receipts, FP32-accumulated FP8 (`use_fast_accum=False`), FP64 Triton RoPE, and mandatory visual quality gating.
- **Where peer frameworks lead**:
  - **NVIDIA SOL Engine**: Hardware-validated **Blackwell B200 NVFP4** measurements (WorldFoundry implements SM100 NVFP4 packing/dispatch but has only hardware-benchmarked Hopper H200 FP8 so far).
  - **SGLang-Diffusion**: Continuous multi-tenant batching, scheduler queuing, and OpenAI-compatible HTTP serving.
  - **Learned Latent Cascades**: Replacing the `1.72s` decoded-frame upscale + LTX VAE encode handoff in MiniMax-H3 Super Acceleration with a trained latent adapter remains future work.

## Quality Engineering and Rejected Presets

- **Blocked — LTX-2.3 Distilled Stage-2 Token Pruning**: Pruning 20–25% of Stage-2 tokens on 3-step distilled LTX-2.3 (`1.60s / 2.91x`, `15.4 dB`) produced sky/canopy rainbow blotches; automatically bypassed when `total_steps < 6`.
- **Blocked — HunyuanVideo-13B `tau=1.25`**: Reached `5.84x` but dropped PSNR to `20.80 dB` with late-frame subject drift; replaced with `tau=1.0 + dense_single=(0,-2,-1)` (`5.25x`, `22.51 dB`).
- **Fixed — Hopper FP22 Drift and FP32 RoPE**: All deep DiT FP8 presets enforce `use_fast_accum=False` (full FP32 WGMMA accumulation, under `3%` latency overhead) and register-resident FP64 Triton RoPE (`13.63x` vs `complex128`).
