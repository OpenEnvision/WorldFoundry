## Methodology and Hardware Disclosures

<Callout type="warn" title="Hardware Precision Disclosure (Hopper H200 FP8 vs Blackwell B200 NVFP4)">
Where NVIDIA SOL Engine reports **Blackwell B200 SM100 NVFP4** numbers, WorldFoundry executes **Hopper SM90 Rowwise FP8 W8A8 (`use_fast_accum=False`, full FP32 WGMMA accumulation)** on bare-metal **NVIDIA H200 SXM (141 GiB HBM3e, SM90, NVSwitch)**. SM100 NVFP4 weight packing and dispatch are implemented and unit-tested in-tree, pending physical B200 hardware benchmarking.
</Callout>
